## Raker Lab "Homemade" TRIzol Recipe
#### For use during coral sample RNA extractions

### Protocol
From [Spoorthy C Bhimanare](https://www.researchgate.net/post/Can-anybody-tell-me-how-to-prepare-the-trizol-reagent-for-total-RNA-isolation) and [Dr. Julian Verdonk](https://postharvestcentral.com/2016/01/23/rna-isolation-with-homemade-trizol-reagent/#)

#### Materials and proportions
* 38% buffer equilibriated Phenol: pH 5 or lower
    * Can also dissolve Phenol crystals in a water bath at ~50C
* 0.8M Guanidine thiocyanate
* 0.4M Ammonium thiocyanate
* 0.1M Sodium acetate: 3M, pH 5 or lower
* 5% Glycerol
* Ultra-pure water to final volume (DEPC treated or other)

#### For 1 litre
* 380mL Phenol
* 94.88g Guanidine thiocyanate
* 30.4488g Ammonium thiocyanate
* 33.4mL Sodium acetate
* 50mL Glycerol
* Raise to 1 litre with ultra pure water

#### For 250mL
* 95mL Phenol
* 23.72g Guanidine thiocyanate
* 7.6122g Ammonium thiocyanate
* 8.35mL Sodium acetate
* 12.5mL Glycerol
* Raise to 250mL with ultra pure water

#### For 100mL
* 38mL Phenol
* 9.488g Guanidine thiocyanate
* 3.04488g Ammonium thiocyanate
* 3.34mL Sodium acetate
* 5mL Glycerol
* Raise to 100mL with ultra pure water
